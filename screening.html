Understanding Screenings

These screenings are clinically validated tools used widely by psychologists worldwide:

  • GAD-7: General Anxiety Disorder score. Scores over 10 warrant checking in with a therapist.
  • PHQ-9: Patient Health Questionnaire score for depression.
  • Stress Index: Measures perceived uncontrollability and overload.
  • Burnout Assessment: Focuses on exhaustion and cynical detachment levels.
Disclaimer: Self-screenings are not diagnostic instruments. If you are experiencing severe emotional distress, please consult a clinical professional.
