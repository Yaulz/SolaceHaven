Sleep Hygiene Insights

The 10-3-2-1-0 Rule
10 hours: No caffeine.
3 hours: No heavy meals or alcohol.
2 hours: No work or studying.
1 hour: No screens.
0 times: Number of times you hit snooze in the morning!
Binaural Beats Explained
Theta frequencies (4Hz to 7Hz) mimic the brainwaves found in deep meditation and REM sleep. Make sure to wear headphones to allow the frequency difference between left and right channels to trigger a relaxing neural entrainment!
Cognitive Shuffling (CBT Technique)
If your mind is racing, pick a word (e.g., 'CALM'). For each letter, think of multiple unrelated words starting with that letter (e.g. Cat, Cloud, Candle...). This scrambled word association breaks the loop of linear, worry-based thoughts and tricks the brain into falling asleep.
